## Page 001 - Contracting Parties and Definitions

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 1.1 Parties

This Laboratory Services and Specimen Logistics Agreement is entered into by Elmbridge Community Health Network (Client) and Northpoint Reference Laboratories LLC (Laboratory), effective 1 June 2027. The organizations serve fictional communities across the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.

### 1.2 Scope of Relationship

Laboratory is an independent contractor performing ordered diagnostic tests and specimen transport services. Client retains responsibility for clinical decisions, patient consent and communicating results to patients. No provision allows Laboratory to practice medicine on behalf of Client.

### 1.3 Defined Terms

A Routine Test is any test listed in the agreed standard catalog without a priority designation; a STAT Test requires urgent processing; a Critical Result is a result meeting the escalation thresholds in Schedule C. Business Day means Monday through Friday excluding Massachusetts public holidays.

## Page 002 - Locations, Testing Catalog and Specimen Limits

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 2.1 Collection Sites

The initial scope covers six Client-operated collection centers identified as EC-01 through EC-06. Laboratory will transport accepted specimens to its central processing facility at a fictional Springfield, Massachusetts location.

### 2.2 Catalog

Schedule A contains 85 approved routine assays and 12 STAT assays at contract signing. Changes to assay codes, collection tubes or specimen volume requirements must be communicated through a revised controlled catalog at least 15 Business Days before implementation, except emergency safety changes.

### 2.3 Specimen Acceptance

Laboratory may reject specimens that are unlabeled, incorrectly matched to the requisition or outside validated transport temperature limits. Rejected specimens must be logged with the rejection reason and reported to the originating site.

## Page 003 - Service Period and Implementation

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 3.1 Term

The initial contract term begins on 1 July 2027 and ends on 30 June 2030. Unless either Party delivers a nonrenewal notice at least 90 days before expiration, the agreement automatically renews for successive one-year terms.

### 3.2 Implementation

During the first 30 calendar days after commencement, Laboratory will validate electronic requisition mappings, courier pickup manifests and critical-result call trees. Client must approve specimen labeling templates before the production switch-over.

### 3.3 Volume Planning

The anticipated test volume is 18,000 specimens per calendar month; this is a planning estimate, not a minimum-purchase guarantee. A change exceeding 25 percent for two consecutive months triggers capacity planning discussions, not an automatic fee change.

## Page 004 - Fee Schedule, Invoice Review and Payment

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 4.1 Prices

The agreed price is USD 12.50 per Routine Test and USD 38.00 per STAT Test, excluding third-party referral assays explicitly approved in advance. Courier service for the six initial sites is included in those test fees.

### 4.2 Invoicing

Laboratory will submit an itemized invoice by the seventh Business Day of the following month, specifying specimen identifier, test code, category and billed amount. Client will pay undisputed amounts net 35 calendar days following receipt.

### 4.3 Audit and Disputes

Client may dispute a billing item within 60 calendar days after invoice receipt. Parties will work from sample-level logs to resolve the issue; a disputed line item does not excuse payment of unrelated undisputed items.

## Page 005 - Pickup Schedules and Chain of Custody

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 5.1 Daily Collections

On regular Business Days, couriers will collect from EC-01 through EC-04 at 11:00 and 17:00 local time; EC-05 and EC-06 receive a single pickup at 16:00. STAT specimens may use the dedicated on-demand dispatch line.

### 5.2 Chain of Custody

Each sealed transport container must carry a unique manifest code, pickup timestamp, courier identifier and temperature-monitor status. Laboratory will acknowledge custody at its receiving station and reconcile any specimen count mismatch within two hours.

### 5.3 Transport Failure

If a scheduled pickup is missed by more than 45 minutes, the logistics coordinator must notify the site and give a revised estimated pickup time. Temperature excursions must be documented and affected specimens quarantined pending an acceptance decision.

## Page 006 - Turnaround Times and Critical Results

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 6.1 Routine Turnaround

At least 95 percent of Routine Test results must be released within 24 hours from laboratory specimen accession, excluding documented rejection and approved send-out tests.

### 6.2 STAT Turnaround

At least 90 percent of STAT Test results must be released within 90 minutes of accession. These turnaround measures are assessed monthly using timestamped accession and release events.

### 6.3 Critical Notification

A confirmed Critical Result must be communicated by telephone to the designated licensed clinician within 20 minutes of confirmation, with read-back documented. If the first contact fails, Laboratory must follow the alternate escalation chain in Schedule C.

## Page 007 - Quality, Regulatory Compliance and Corrective Action

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 7.1 Standards

Laboratory must maintain applicable CLIA certification and any required Massachusetts laboratory licenses. Client may request proof of current certifications no more than quarterly except after a material regulatory notice.

### 7.2 Quality Events

Laboratory will notify Client within one Business Day of discovering a testing error that may materially affect previously released patient results and will provide an interim risk assessment with affected identifiers.

### 7.3 Improvement Plans

If monthly Routine Test turnaround falls below 95 percent in two consecutive months, Laboratory must submit a corrective action plan within ten Business Days after the second monthly report, including root cause, owner and targeted completion date.

## Page 008 - Privacy, Security and Records

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 8.1 Protected Data

Both parties must comply with applicable privacy laws and their separately executed business associate agreement where required. Laboratory may use patient information only to fulfill ordered services, legal obligations and permitted quality functions.

### 8.2 Security Incident

Laboratory must notify Client of a confirmed unauthorized disclosure of patient information within 24 hours after confirmation, and cooperate in documenting scope, containment and legally required notification activities.

### 8.3 Retention and Disposal

Laboratory will retain test reports and associated accession records for seven years, unless a longer mandatory period applies. Physical specimen disposal must follow validated biosafety protocols and applicable retention rules.

## Page 009 - Service Credits, Liability and Termination

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 9.1 Service Credits

If monthly Routine Test turnaround is below 95 percent, Client may claim a service credit equal to 3 percent of that month's Routine Test charges; below 90 percent, the credit is 6 percent. The credit tiers are not cumulative.

### 9.2 Liability Allocation

Except for fraud, willful misconduct and breaches of confidentiality or patient-data security, each Party's aggregate contractual liability is capped at the amount paid or payable under this agreement during the previous twelve months.

### 9.3 Termination

A material breach that remains uncured 30 calendar days after written notice permits termination. Either Party may terminate immediately if a regulatory suspension makes performance of the core testing services unlawful.

## Page 010 - Transition, Notice and Governing Law

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 10.1 Transition Support

For 60 calendar days following termination or expiration, Laboratory will provide reasonable cooperation to transfer outstanding orders, approved result history and specimen tracking records to Client or a replacement provider, subject to patient-data safeguards.

### 10.2 Formal Notice

Formal contractual notice is effective when delivered by tracked courier to the contracting offices listed in the signature block. Email may accompany the notice but does not replace delivery for a notice of termination.

### 10.3 Governing Law and Dispute Process

Massachusetts law governs. The Parties will first refer billing and service disputes to their contract managers for ten Business Days, then escalate unresolved disputes to executive representatives before filing litigation in courts located in Suffolk County, Massachusetts.
