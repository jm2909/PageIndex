### 1.1 Parties

This Colocation and Managed Facilities Agreement is entered into by Cedar Peak Infrastructure LLC (Provider) and Glasshaven Analytics Inc. (Customer), each a Party and together the Parties. The Effective Date is 15 February 2027. Provider operates the Cedar Peak Data Campus in fictional Briarfield, Colorado; Customer requires a managed environment for its production analytics workloads.

### 1.2 Contract Documents

The executed order form, this agreement, Service Schedule A, Security Schedule B and the signed change orders form the contract. If terms conflict, the signed change order controls only the specifically changed subject, followed by the order form, this agreement and the schedules. Marketing descriptions do not vary an express service commitment.

### 1.3 Designated Contacts

Provider designates its Facilities Operations Manager as the operational contact and its Legal Notices Office as the contractual contact. Customer designates its Infrastructure Director as technical contact and General Counsel as contractual contact. A change in contact does not amend any financial or service obligation.

## Page 002 - Licensed Space and Authorized Equipment

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 2.1 Space Allocation

Provider grants Customer a contractual license to use Suite D-14 containing twelve secured equipment racks in Building DC-2; the license is not a lease of real property. Each rack has a nominal usable capacity of 42 rack units, subject to safe installation and equipment clearance rules.

### 2.2 Power and Connectivity

The initial committed power allocation is 96 kW aggregate across the suite, with dual A/B feeds. Customer may order up to four diverse carrier cross-connects through a signed order. No cross-connect is active solely by reserving its port.

### 2.3 Access Boundaries

Provider retains control of all corridors, mechanical rooms, network meet-me rooms and emergency exits. Customer may install or remove listed equipment only in its allocated racks and cannot expand into neighboring cabinets without written authorization.

## Page 003 - Term, Acceptance and Renewal

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 3.1 Initial Term

The initial service term is 36 months beginning on the Service Commencement Date of 1 April 2027 and ending on 31 March 2030, unless terminated earlier under this agreement. The parties will use an acceptance checklist for power-feed, rack-lock and network-handoff readiness.

### 3.2 Acceptance Process

Customer has five Business Days after Provider issues the Ready for Service notice to test the delivered configuration. Customer will describe any material deficiency in writing. If no material deficiency is reported in that window, the service is deemed accepted.

### 3.3 Renewal

Customer may renew for one additional 24-month term by giving at least 120 calendar days written notice before initial expiration. Renewal rates will be the then-current published list price less a negotiated 8 percent discount, unless a signed renewal order specifies otherwise.

## Page 004 - Fees, Billing and Security

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 4.1 Recurring Charges

The monthly colocation license charge is USD 18,600. Included in that fee are twelve racks and up to 96 kW of reserved power capacity, but measured electricity consumption is billed separately at USD 0.14 per kWh. Cross-connects are USD 175 each per month.

### 4.2 Payment and Deposit

Invoices are issued on the first Business Day of each calendar month, payable net 30 days from receipt. Customer must maintain a refundable security deposit of USD 37,200, which may be applied to undisputed overdue charges after written notice and a ten-day cure opportunity.

### 4.3 Annual Adjustment

Beginning 1 April 2028, the recurring license charge increases by a fixed 3 percent on each service anniversary. Power usage rates may change only with 60 days prior notice and documented change in Provider energy costs.

## Page 005 - Service Levels and Outage Credits

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 5.1 Availability Target

Provider commits to 99.95 percent monthly availability of contracted power at the rack power-distribution-unit demarcation point, excluding approved scheduled maintenance and exclusions described in this section.

### 5.2 Incident Severity

A complete loss of both A and B power feeds to Customer racks is Severity 1 and must receive an engineer acknowledgement within 15 minutes of ticket creation. A single-feed degradation is Severity 2 and must receive an acknowledgement within 60 minutes.

### 5.3 Credit Mechanism

For each full 30 minutes of qualifying monthly power unavailability beyond the allowed downtime, Customer receives a credit equal to 2 percent of the monthly license charge, capped at 20 percent of that charge per month. Customer must submit a credit claim within 20 calendar days after the affected month.

## Page 006 - Physical Security and Visitor Controls

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 6.1 Facility Controls

Provider will maintain continuous CCTV coverage of common security corridors, badge access logs and a staffed security desk at all times. CCTV recordings must be retained for at least 90 days, except when preserved longer for an incident or legal hold.

### 6.2 Authorized Personnel

Customer may nominate up to eight named authorized technicians for unescorted access to Suite D-14. Additional visitors require 24 hours advance registration and must be escorted by Provider personnel while inside controlled areas.

### 6.3 Security Events

Provider must notify Customer within four hours after confirming unauthorized physical access to Customer cabinets. The notice will identify known scope, immediate containment actions and the designated incident coordinator without making unsupported root-cause claims.

## Page 007 - Maintenance, Change Windows and Remote Hands

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 7.1 Maintenance Window

Routine facility maintenance that may affect service is scheduled for Sunday from 02:00 to 06:00 Mountain Time, with not less than seven calendar days prior notice. Emergency maintenance may occur outside that window when needed to prevent imminent equipment damage or safety risk.

### 7.2 Remote Hands

Provider includes eight hours of remote-hands assistance per calendar month. Additional approved assistance is billed at USD 110 per hour in 15-minute increments, with a one-hour minimum per discrete request.

### 7.3 Customer Change Control

Customer must submit a method statement 48 hours before changes that materially alter cabling, power draw or rack footprint. Provider may reject a proposed change if it reasonably threatens cooling, fire protection or neighboring customers.

## Page 008 - Data Responsibility and Confidentiality

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 8.1 Data Boundary

Customer retains ownership and operational control of its applications and stored data. Provider does not receive authority to inspect Customer content merely because the equipment resides on Provider premises. Provider manages building-level services and does not provide application backup under this agreement.

### 8.2 Confidential Information

Each Party must use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform this agreement and protect it with at least reasonable care. Ordinary confidentiality obligations survive for three years after expiration; trade secrets remain protected for so long as they qualify under applicable law.

### 8.3 Disclosures

A compelled legal disclosure is permitted only to the extent legally required, with prompt advance notice where law allows. Receiving Party must not disclose access credentials to subcontractors who lack a documented need to know.

## Page 009 - Liability, Insurance and Termination

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 9.1 Liability Cap

Except for fraud, willful misconduct, unpaid fees and confidentiality breaches, each Party's aggregate liability under the agreement is capped at twelve months of recurring license charges paid or payable immediately before the claim.

### 9.2 Insurance

Provider must carry commercial general liability insurance with USD 2 million per occurrence and cyber liability insurance of USD 5 million aggregate. Customer must carry property coverage for its own equipment; Provider does not insure Customer hardware.

### 9.3 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate for a material breach remaining uncured 30 days after written notice. Nonpayment of undisputed invoices has a separate 15-day cure period after a written default notice.

## Page 010 - Exit, Notices and Governing Law

<!-- PAGE_BREAK -->

### 10.1 Equipment Removal

Customer must remove its equipment within 20 Business Days after the service ends and restore racks to a safe, clean condition. Provider will offer supervised access for that purpose and may charge reasonable documented storage fees after the removal period.

### 10.2 Formal Notices

Formal notices must be sent by recognized overnight courier or tracked registered mail to the legal addresses in the order form, with an email courtesy copy. Operational tickets and email alone are not valid contractual termination notices.

### 10.3 Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. Exclusive venue for contractual disputes is the state or federal courts located in Denver, Colorado. The parties may seek urgent injunctive relief where legally appropriate.
